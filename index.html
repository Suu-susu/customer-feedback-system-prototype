Tait Hub Customer Feedback System Prototype

A self-directed UX/UI and web platform concept project by Jiashuo Su, created for Tait's Junior Design Engineer Summer Intern role. The project explores how customer feedback can be collected at the point of use, structured for triage, and connected back to product improvement.

Case study

UX/UI Web platform Customer feedback Prototype Triage workflow

Problem

Users of technical platforms often notice documentation gaps, confusing workflows, or product issues while they are trying to complete a task. If feedback is buried in a generic support channel, product teams may receive comments without enough page context, severity, or reproducible detail.

Design goal

Create a lightweight feedback system that is fast for the user, structured enough for support triage, and useful for product and engineering teams.

Target users

  • Field technicians and operational users who need quick feedback submission.
  • Support staff who need severity, page context, and clear notes for triage.
  • Product and UX teams who need patterns across feedback, not isolated comments.
  • Engineers who need reproducible information before investigating issues.

Prototype flow

1Open feedback from the current product or support page.
2Select feedback type and severity to structure the report.
3Add a short note, optional contact, and automatic page context.
4Submit and receive a confirmation reference.
5Route feedback into a triage dashboard for product and support teams.

Validation plan

  • Ask 3-5 users to submit sample feedback from a technical page.
  • Observe where users hesitate, choose the wrong category, or write vague notes.
  • Check whether support/product stakeholders can triage reports from the fields alone.
  • Iterate labels, categories, and confirmation text based on test findings.
